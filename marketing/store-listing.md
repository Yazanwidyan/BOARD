# ReelBoard — store listing

The pitch, in one line: **rank every movie you watch from S to F, and let your
tier list decide what you watch next.** Tier lists are the hook (they're what
people share); deciding tonight's movie is the daily reason to open the app.

Paste-ready copy for App Store Connect and Google Play. Character limits are
noted next to each field; every field below is within them.

## Before you publish the name

These need you, not code:

- [ ] **Trademark search** for "ReelBoard" and "Reel Board" in the classes for
      software and entertainment (Nice classes 9, 41 and 42): USPTO (US),
      EUIPO (EU), WIPO Global Brand Database, plus your home country's office.
- [ ] **Store search** for "ReelBoard" on the App Store and Google Play — a
      same-name app in a related category is a rejection or a dispute risk.
- [ ] **Domain**: check reelboard.app / reelboard.com / getreelboard.com and
      register one (the app's privacy policy and support pages need a URL).
- [ ] **Handles**: @reelboard (or @reelboardapp) on Instagram, TikTok and X —
      tier lists are made for those feeds.
- [ ] **Privacy policy and support URL** — both stores require them; the
      account holds email, profile, watch history and friends.
- [ ] **Data safety (Play) / privacy labels (App Store)**: account info, user
      content (lists, bio, photo), identifiers (push token); not sold, not used
      for ads.
- [ ] **Movie data attribution**: posters and ratings come from OMDb. If you
      add TMDB for where-to-watch, its terms require "This product uses the
      TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB" plus their logo in
      the app's About.
